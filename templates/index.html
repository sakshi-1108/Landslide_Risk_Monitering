🧠 RL Policy Lab experimental

A small reinforcement-learning experiment that is separate from the live alert and does not change it. A Q-learning agent learns, from historical daily rainfall and recorded landslides, when a district should be on No alert, Watch or Warning. It is then replayed on later years it never saw and compared with simple policies.

State
yesterday's rain, 3-day and 7-day rain (binned) and the current alert level
Actions
No alert, Watch, Warning (chosen every day)
Reward
minus alert cost, minus switching cost, minus a large penalty for a missed landslide

Results appear after running the two RL scripts once (see below).

Policy comparison on unseen years

Cost is in reward units per district-year (lower is better); the cost numbers are design assumptions.

Sensitivity to the missed-landslide penalty

The learned policy depends on how bad a missed landslide is judged to be. All runs are shown, not only the favourable one.
